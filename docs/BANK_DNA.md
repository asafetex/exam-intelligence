# Bank DNA / Assessment DNA

## Two distinct DNAs

### Content DNA
What a bank tends to test: topics, propositions, legal devices, recurrence and depth.

### Assessment DNA
How the bank turns knowledge into items: stem operators, distractor types, wording transformations, exceptions, multi-statement structures and scoring behavior.

Do not collapse these into simple frequency tables.

## Contextualization

Bank patterns are estimated in context such as:

```text
Atomic Topic × Bank × Role/Area × Organization/Exam Family × Period
```

Shrink toward broader priors when data is sparse. Do not claim a bank-specific rule from a handful of items.

## Candidate-facing value

Bank DNA should eventually answer questions like:
- Which propositions does this bank repeatedly reuse?
- Which distractor transformations does it favor?
- Does the candidate know the content but fail the bank's wording style?
- Which legal devices/articles are repeatedly targeted?
- Which topic families are stable across time vs recent/emerging?

## Trap DNA

Later, question/distractor labels can expose candidate-bank interactions, e.g.:

```text
VUNESP + rule_exception → candidate error rate 2.1× baseline
CEBRASPE + absolutization → high confident-wrong rate
```

This can motivate targeted trap drills rather than more generic theory.

## Prediction guardrail

Bank DNA produces features/evidence, not claims that an exact item “will fall.” Historical recurrence, recency and stability may increase expected importance but never remove uncertainty.
