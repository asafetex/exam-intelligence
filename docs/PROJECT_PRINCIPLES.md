# Project Principles

## P1 — Decision quality over platform completeness
Build the smallest capability that improves the next study decision. Platform features that do not affect diagnosis, intervention or answer performance are secondary.

## P2 — Evidence lineage by default
Every important conclusion should be traceable to its source: exam occurrence, answer-key version, syllabus item, candidate attempt, learning source or model/version.

## P3 — Measurement before sophistication
Simple, interpretable baselines precede advanced models. Complex mastery/forecast models must beat simpler alternatives on held-out or prospective evidence.

## P4 — Performance is not learning
Immediate correctness is not durable learning. Retention and delayed validation are separate evidence channels.

## P5 — Coverage-aware mastery
A subject-level percentage is not mastery when the tested sample is narrow or unbalanced. Breadth and atomic-topic coverage must influence certainty.

## P6 — Bank behavior is contextual
Historical frequency, recurrence, distractors and interpretation patterns are modeled by bank × role/organ × period × topic where data supports it. No bank stereotype becomes a timeless law.

## P7 — Repetition can be signal or noise
Source duplicates and alternate cadernos are noise. Legitimate reuse of the same canonical question in distinct exams is evidence. Semantic-family recurrence and topic incidence are distinct evidence layers.

## P8 — Official scoring decision is not universal truth
Store what the bank accepted, when, under which key. Scientific/legal truth and current authority live in separate evidence structures.

## P9 — Learning methods are interventions, not identities
OQF, Benites, EARA, spacing, retrieval, flashcards and other methods are decomposed into interventions matched to diagnosed problems. No guru/method is assumed universally optimal.

## P10 — Personalization is learned, not claimed
User-specific method effects require repeated comparable evidence/N-of-1 experiments; the system must not manufacture “personalization” from sparse data.

## P11 — Source agnostic, contract centered
The internal canonical model must not depend on Quest API, a particular site export or a particular PDF layout.

## P12 — Local/private by default
Raw proprietary course material, candidate evidence and restricted corpora do not belong in a public Git repository.

## P13 — Append, do not rewrite history
Attempts, key versions, source assets, migrations and historical legal versions are append/version oriented. Later knowledge must not silently rewrite the past.

## P14 — Uncertainty is a feature
Report insufficient coverage, low sample size, low parser confidence and model uncertainty explicitly instead of presenting false precision.
